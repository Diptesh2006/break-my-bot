# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

Maintain a professional tone in issues, reviews, and community spaces. Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools.
